Audio measurement workspace

Capture sound. See what is happening.

Record measurements, monitor a room in real time, inspect waveforms, and compare detailed acoustic analysis in one focused workspace.

Measure

Three ways to work

01

Acquisition

Play a swept-sine stimulus through a chosen output and record the response with optional reference input. Captures are saved as WAV files and opened for analysis.

02

Live

Compare microphone and reference inputs continuously. Follow frequency response, level difference, and delay alignment while sound is running.

03

SPL mode

Track calibrated sound pressure level with selectable A or C weighting, response speed, averaging, and a rolling 60-second history.

Inspect

Waveform view

Work directly with the recording

Zoom into time and amplitude, choose visible channels, inspect the spectrogram, and play selected channels through your preferred output.

Analysis view

Compare acoustic behavior

Review frequency and phase response, impulse alignment, distortion, THD, coherence, and phase delay across one or several recordings.

Files

Upload one or more WAV files and open waveform tabs or combined analysis tabs.
